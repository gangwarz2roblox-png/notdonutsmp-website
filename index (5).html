Setup

Add it to your game.

Minecraft 1.21.11, Fabric and Fabric API. Follow the setup guide for the full instructions.

Full setup guide ↗

  1. Get your download

    After your purchase is activated, sign in and download the client from your dashboard.

  2. Add the client to Fabric

    Place the .jar file in .minecraft/mods alongside Fabric API.

  3. Open your client

    Launch Minecraft with Fabric. Press Right Shift to choose your modules and settings.
